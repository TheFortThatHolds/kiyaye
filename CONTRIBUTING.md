# Contributing

## Adding Content

- Verified content: include verification details
- Unverified content: mark clearly as unverified
- Community consent required for endangered language materials

## Pull Requests

Open one. It'll get reviewed.

## Issues

Open one with enough detail to understand the problem.

## Licensing

This project is CC0 1.0 — public domain. By opening a pull request you place
your contribution in the public domain under the same terms, waiving any
attribution or share-alike claim over it. Do not contribute material you
cannot release this way — including anything a language community has not
consented to releasing.

## What Not to Contribute

- Content without community consent
- Sacred or restricted knowledge
- Unverified content labeled as verified
